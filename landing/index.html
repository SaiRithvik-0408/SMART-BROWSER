What you get

Privacy isn't an extension. It's the browser.

Built-in multi-region Tor

Five preconfigured exit regions (Anywhere, US, Germany, Netherlands, France) shipped as part of the app. No accounts, no monthly fees.

IP & URL masking

Outbound traffic carries the Tor exit's IP. Inbound URLs are rewritten through a reverse-proxy so the network only ever sees one origin.

Real Chromium DevTools, docked

Right-click → Inspect opens DevTools on the right side, exactly like Chrome. You can also pop it out into its own window.
